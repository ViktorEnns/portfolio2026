# **🎭 Comedy OS: Notation System (V1.1)**

creation date: 3. Aug 2026

This system provides a high-speed, lightweight notation language designed for rapid transcription and live set-list tagging. It captures vocal dynamics, delivery cadence, physical movement, and facial expressions without interrupting creative flow.

## **📝 Complete Script Example in Action**

§ I went to the DMV yesterday... _ and the guy behind the counter looked at me like I stole his car. :disgust §  
~D [ Shift into counter guy character ]  
" Next in line! " + //slouches  
# - I walked up and asked, ~ "How's your day going?" ~ **💥** v and the clerk asked if I brought my own survival supplies. # __  
% 😐 [lean on mic stand, long stare into the audience] + He wasn't joking. He handed me a water purification tablet. % [hold]  
<< -- Pretty sure that man hasn't slept since 2012. >> _ #!

## **🧱 1. Joke Structural Markers**

These structural wrappers isolate setup, delivery, punchlines, tags, and internal monologue into distinct, machine-parseable objects.

| Symbol / Wrapper | Structural Element | Function / Description | Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| § text § | **Setup** | The premise, context, or foundation of the joke. | § I went to the DMV yesterday... § |
| ~D or [deliv] | **Delivery Note** | Instruction on vocal tone, character shift, or execution. | ~D [deadpan, slow roll] |
| # text # | **Punchline (Regular)** | Standard punchline delivery / core subversion. | # and the clerk asked if I brought my own survival supplies. # |
| #! text # | **Punchline (Emphasized)** | Punchline delivered with strong emphasis or extra punch. | #! and he handed me a gas mask! # |
| #!! text # | **Punchline (Over the Top)** | Maximum intensity, loud, screaming, or unhinged punchline. | #!! WHO IS RELAXING?! # |
| % text % | **Tag / Topper** | An additional punchline or continuation building directly on the premise. | % He wasn't joking. He handed me a water purification tablet. % |
| << text >> | **Aside / Internal Monologue** | Stage whisper, self-break, or out-of-character observation directed to self or crowd. | << Pretty sure that man hasn't slept since 2012. >> |

## **⏱️ 2. Pacing & Timing**

| Symbol | Action | Description / Guidance | Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| _ | **Micro-Pause / Beat** | ***1 lower dash*** ~1 second breath or hesitation. | *I called my mom... _ she didn't answer.* |
| __ | **Medium Pause** | ***2 lower dashes*** 2–3 second beat for setup assembly. | *So I waited. __ And waited.* |
| ___ | **Long Pause / Stare-Down** | ***3 lower dashes*** 3+ seconds; intentional tension building. | *She looked at me. ___ Pure hatred.* |
| [hold] | **Crowd Hold** | Pausing delivery to wait out laughs or applause. | *...and that was my uncle! **P!** [hold]* |

## **🔊 3. Dynamics & Pitch**

| Symbol | Action | Description / Guidance | Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| = | **Normal Volume** | Standard speaking delivery baseline. | *= I went to the store.* |
| + | **Projection / Loud** | Elevated stage voice; emphasized delivery. | *+ Why are you doing that?!* |
| ++ | **Yell / Scream** | Maximum vocal intensity or shrieking. | *++ GET OUT OF HERE!* |
| - | **Quiet / Soft** | Dropping volume to draw the audience in. | *- I turned around and whispered...* |
| -- | **Whisper** | Barely audible / confidential delivery. | *-- Don't look behind you.* |
| ^ | **Pitch Shift Up** | High-pitched voice, inflection, or squeak. | *^ Are you serious right now?* |
| v | **Pitch Shift Down** | Low, deep, or gravelly vocal drop. | *v I don't think so, man.* |

## **🎵 4. Vocal Texture & Expression**

| Symbol | Action | Description / Guidance | Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 💥 | **Emphasis** | Heavy accentuation on a specific word. | *I told him 💥NEVER to do that.* |
| P! | **Punchline** | Marks the exact moment the punchline lands. | *Turns out, I was the doctor. **P!*** |
| ~ | **Sarcasm / Elongation** | Drawn-out vowels or sarcastic inflection. | *Oh, ~ greeeat job, genius.* |
| * | **Mouth Noise / Flavor** | Tongue click, sigh, lip smack, throat clear. | ** [sigh] Anyway...* |
| " | **Character Voice** | Shift into an impression, accent, or roleplay. | *" Ay, fugheddaboudit! "* |
| < > | **Internal Monologue / Aside** | Stage whisper or thought breaking character. | *< Why did I say that out loud? >* |
| 👫 | **Multi-Character Dialogue** | Marks a quick back-and-forth between roles. | *👫 [Doctor vs. Patient interaction]* |

## **🤸 5. Act-Outs & Stage Directions**

**Note:** Fast-entry slash commands (//) are used for inline typing. Stage direction brackets ([]) are reserved for longer spatial notes.

| Symbol / Syntax | Action | Description / Guidance | Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| //[action] | **Inline Act-Out** | Single quick physical action embedded directly in a line. | //crabwalk *across the stage* |
| [text] | **Stage Direction** | Multi-step physical placement, movement, or posture shift. | [lean on mic stand, long stare into the audience] |
| 🤸 | **Physicality Emoji** | Visual tag for physical slapstick or heavy movement. | 🤸 [trips over mic cord] |
| @ | **Spatial Position** | Stage positioning or movement direction. | @ center stage, @ stool |

## **🤪 6. Facial Expressions**

| Symbol / Syntax | Action | Description / Guidance | Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| :expression | **Facial Expression Tag** | Fast typographic tag for specific performance faces. | :smirk, :disgust, :deadpan |
| 🤪 | **Facial Emoji Marker** | Visual indicator for wild or exaggerated expressions. | 🤪 [manic grin] |
| 😐 | **Deadpan / Blank** | Cold, expressionless stare during a hold. | 😐 [stares silently for 5 seconds] |
| 😬 | **Wince / Cringe** | Reaction face for awkward premises or unexpected reactions. | 😬 [pained reaction] |

## **🚀 Future Roadmap Additions (Method Integrations)**

### **🧘 1. The Box Breathing Method (Pacing Architecture)**

> * **Concept:** Structural rhythm based on cyclical tension and release (Yoga Box Breathing applied to comedy mechanics).  
> * **Pattern:** Premise → Hold → Punchline → Hold → Tag → Hold → Transition.  
> * **Notation:**  
  * [in] Setup / Air in  
  * [hold-t] Holding tension before punch  
  * [out] Punch release  
  * [hold-l] Holding for laugh

### **🎙️ 2. The Tony Hinchcliffe Method (Story Progression & Interrogation)**

> * **Concept:** Rapid-fire character & narrative extraction driven by conversational prompts (*"And then what happened?"*).  
> * **Notation & Prompts:**  
  * ?next Prompts the AI/Writer: *"And then what happened?"*  
  * ?who Prompts character detail: *"What did they look/sound like?"*  
  * ?feel Prompts internal state: *"How did you feel at that exact second?"*  
  * ?pivot Detects buried ledes or drifting tangents to double back to the core premise.
